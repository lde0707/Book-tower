## 생성 프롬프트

Use case: illustration-story. Create a production game texture atlas, a single square image divided into exactly 2x2 equal square quadrants with no borders or labels. All four quadrants have the SAME pale warm ivory handmade mulberry paper background with delicate visible paper fibers, evenly lit flat scanned surface, no perspective, no shadows or 3D scene. Entire image is hand-drawn wax crayon on paper with irregular grainy strokes, charming sophisticated children's picturebook craft, muted terracotta coral and desaturated teal plus ochre. Top-left quadrant: a full-body cute sitting rabbit facing right, large ears, rounded silhouette drawn in loose coral crayon, small botanical teal sprigs around it, rabbit occupies central 65% with generous clear margins. Top-right quadrant: a cute friendly sitting fox facing forward, wide pointed ears, fluffy cheeks, hand-drawn coral crayon fur hatching, small ochre five-point star centered on its chest, surrounded by sparse teal pressed leaves, fox occupies central 65% with clear margins. Bottom-left quadrant: a full little bird in side view facing left, teal crayon feathers, rounded body, ochre beak and feet, a few leafy branches around it, central 65% with clear margins. Bottom-right quadrant: delicate repeating scattered childlike five-point crayon stars, flowers and leafy twigs in muted coral teal and ochre on the same ivory paper, varied organic imperfect handdrawn shapes, no animal. Each quadrant must be self-contained, nothing crossing the center seams. High quality natural wax grain, subtle erased areas, handcrafted and tender. Avoid flat vector art, polygon shapes, glossy surface, photoreal animals, typography, numbers, hard black outlines, drop shadows. This will be UV-mapped on the walls and barrel ceiling of an indoor paper puzzle game.
